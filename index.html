LAUNCH SCANNER
BIOMETRICS / SIGNAL PROCESSING / COMPUTER VISION 2026

HRV-M CardioCam — Optical Vitals Scanner

A browser-native remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) vitals scanner that measures heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV SDNN), and respiration rate in real time using a standard webcam. Extracts subtle pulse-induced skin micro-color variations across facial regions of interest.

Facial Mesh Tracking: 468-point real-time landmark tracking using Google MediaPipe in WebAssembly.
Optical rPPG Signal Processing: Green-channel spatial averaging, Butterworth filtering, and peak detection.
Zero-Server Privacy: Entire optical measurement executes in client-side memory; video never leaves the device.
